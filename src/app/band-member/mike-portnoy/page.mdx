## Biography

Mike Portnoy was the original drummer and co-founder of Dream Theater, known for his:

- Complex polyrhythmic patterns
- Conceptual album storytelling
- Innovative drumming techniques

## Career Highlights

- Recorded 10 studio albums with Dream Theater
- Won 30+ Modern Drummer Reader Poll awards
- Pioneered the "guitar hero" approach to drumming
